## The Invisible Leak in Everyday Creator Files

Sharing a weekend twisty GPX route or exporting high-resolution photos often leaks sensitive details: exact driveway coordinates, departure timestamps, and hardware serial numbers embedded inside EXIF blocks.

## Two Automated Build Gates on This Site

Instead of relying on manual checks, this site treats OpSec as part of the build itself:

1. **±5 km GPX Sanitization (`sanitize-gpx.mjs`):** Every raw route is measured via cumulative Haversine distance; the first and last **5 km** plus all `<time>` tags are stripped automatically before assets enter `public/gpx/`.
2. **EXIF-Stripped Sharp Pipeline & Post-Build Audit (`audit-opsec.mjs`):** Images preserve orientation and ICC color profiles (`.rotate().keepIccProfile()`) while discarding GPS and serial tags — verified across `dist/` before packaging.
