## Theory Before Class, Real Troubleshooting Together

Traditional slide-heavy lectures on subnetting, Active Directory permissions, or container bridges feel productive until a student sits alone in front of a misconfigured terminal. Moving baseline theory into compact prep materials frees up lab time for what actually turns students into engineers: **structured troubleshooting**.

## Three Habits Across 1ITF & 1ACS Labs

1. **Start with a broken topology:** Students retain far more when fixing one subtle missing route or container namespace rule than blindly typing a happy-path tutorial.
2. **Use coaching questions instead of fast answers:** Asking which OSI layer stopped answering builds lasting mental models.
3. **Verify configurations automatically:** Writing automated verification scripts turns "it works on my laptop" into reproducible engineering discipline.
