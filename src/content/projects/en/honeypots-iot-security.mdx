## Why Build Custom Honeypots?

In cybersecurity courses at Thomas More IT Factory, nothing teaches threat awareness faster than watching real internet background noise hit an exposed lab sensor within minutes. **PotHead** and **ESP32-Public-Honeypot** turn that noise into structured educational telemetry inside safely isolated segments.

- **PotHead (PHP):** Captures automated HTTP exploit probes and login attempts without exposing real application stacks.
- **ESP32-Public-Honeypot (C++):** Proves on bare-metal microcontroller hardware how aggressively automated scanners target IoT TCP/UDP ports.
