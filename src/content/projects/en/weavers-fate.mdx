## The Core Concept: Bluffing Over Transparent Math

*Weavers Fate* grew from the exact same curiosity that drives my engineering lectures: how do humans make decisions under uncertainty when cold probability collides with table psychology?

Around the table, **3 to 5 players** share **6 dice cups** and **28 six-sided dice (D6)**. Alongside each player's hidden dice sits the central **Cup of Fate** — a shared unknown pool that introduces just enough variance so pure counting never guarantees safety.

## Dual Tension: 16 Souls & the Chaos Counter

- **16 Souls Dual Economy:** Every player starts with 16 Souls. Challenging a bluff or losing a call directly drains your health pool, yet those same Souls are what you risk to unlock aggressive plays. Playing overly conservatively starves your options; overcommitting leaves you exposed.
- **Chaos Counter:** Escalating rounds raise the Chaos Counter, raising the stakes and preventing stale defensive stalls.
- **Six Signature Power-Up Cards:**
  1. **The Void** — cancels a threat or alters the resolution state cleanly.
  2. **The Prison** — locks down an opponent's move at a pivotal turn.
  3. **Freeze** — holds key dice state intact across a decisive challenge.
  4. **Second Chance** — offers one last lifeline right before Souls are lost.
  5. **Re-roll** — shakes up a bad roll before the table is forced to call.
  6. **Powerless** — temporarily strips card interference from a rival player.
